    Keeping your key safe

    Anyone who can open this browser on your phone could use the key. Set a monthly spend limit in the Anthropic Console, and never paste your key into the site’s code if you host it publicly.

    Your notes

    Notes are saved in this browser on this device.

    Backups are saved as a .json file, flashcards included. Keep it in Files or iCloud Drive, then use Restore on this or another device. Restoring adds the notes in the backup and keeps the newest version of any note you already have. Restore also accepts the older text backups and text exports.
